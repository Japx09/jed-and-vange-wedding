two separate stories woven by grace into one...

our story

Chapter 1: “Where it all began”

What began with a simple introduction slowly became conversations, shared moments, unexpected trips, and a friendship that grew with intention. Looking back, what felt ordinary was God quietly weaving our stories together.

Chapter 2: “When it became intentional”

Somewhere along the way, the casual conversations became intentional ones. Friendship grew, intentions were made clear, and the decision to pursue one another unfolded, not rushed, but with prayer, purpose, and grace. Looking back, we see how God was quietly leading us, one step at a time.

Chapter 3: “When we said yes to a lifetime”

After all the little moments, unexpected turns, and intentional steps came the question and a resounding yes. Not because we know everything the future holds, but because we trust the God who brought us this far. And now, by His grace, we say yes, not just to each other, but to a lifetime of choosing, growing, serving, and walking with God together.
