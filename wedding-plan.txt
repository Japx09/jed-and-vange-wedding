OUR STORY
	Chapter 1: “Where it all began”
What began with a simple introduction slowly became conversations, shared moments, unexpected trips, and a friendship that grew with intention.
Looking back, what felt ordinary was God quietly weaving our stories together.
Chapter 2: “When it became intentional”
Somewhere along the way, the casual conversations became intentional ones. Friendship grew, intentions were made clear, and the decision to pursue one another unfolded, not rushed, but with prayer, purpose, and grace. 
Looking back, we see how God was quietly leading us, one step at a time. 
Chapter 3: “When we said yes to a lifetime”


After all the little moments, unexpected turns, and intentional steps came the question and a resounding yes. Not because we know everything the future holds, but because we trust the God who brought us this far. And now, by His grace, we say yes, not just to each other, but to a lifetime of choosing, growing, serving, and walking with God together.

* Please refer to our Dress Code section for the suggested attire and color palette.
3. Can I bring a plus-one?
* We kindly ask that you follow the number of guests indicated in your invitation.
4. Are children invited?
* Please refer to your invitation for the guests included in your RSVP.
5. Is parking available?
* Yes, parking is available at the venue. Our coordinators and ushers will be happy to assist you upon arrival.

* Please let us know when you RSVP so we can make the necessary arrangements.
