Attire Etiquette & Reminders

  • Please avoid wearing all-white, ivory, or cream dresses reserved for the bride.
  • Kindly avoid neon colors, very bright red, orange, yellow, or overly loud prints.
  • We request no denim jeans, shorts, and casual footwear (rubber slippers or sneakers).
  • Garden setting: ladies are encouraged to wear comfortable block heels, wedges, or dressy flats.
